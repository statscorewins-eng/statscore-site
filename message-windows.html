Window Doctrine
Always Open Support Lanes Coach, counselor, evaluator, and program support lanes remain available through governed system channels.
Parent-Gated Recruiter Contact Minor athlete contact from recruiters must route through guardian-controlled approval windows.
NCAA-Safe Messaging Recruiter contact timing must be logged, governed, and reviewed against allowed communication context.
Pause / Freeze Authority Parent, admin, or governance review can pause visibility or communication windows when required.
Receipts Required Every window opening, closing, restriction, approval, and escalation requires a traceable receipt.
