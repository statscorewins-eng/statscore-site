PHNX Sports Rankings

PHNX Sports publishes authorized ranking intelligence produced by the governing STATS-CORE™ intelligence and ranking authorities. Stream 7 preserves ranking criteria, authority, version, explainability, visibility governance, history, and publication evidence without calculating the underlying score or official rank.

Ranking Intelligence ≠ Ranking Publication. Stream 9 / the designated governing authority produces ranking intelligence. Stream 7 publishes it. Exposure/Viewership rankings never become evidence of athletic ability merely because they appear on PHNX Sports.

Leaderboard Classes

Classes remain constitutionally distinct
Athletic Publishes governed Athletic Intelligence rankings only. Stream 7 does not determine Athletic Score or ranking science.
Academic Publishes governed Academic Intelligence rankings without converting academic standing into athletic ability.
Development Publishes governed longitudinal Development Intelligence rankings where the ranking authority has established improvement criteria.
Verified Performance Publishes rankings based upon declared verified-performance criteria supplied by the governing authority.
Exposure / Viewership Publishes governed visibility or engagement rankings. Exposure and engagement do not establish athlete ability.
Program Publishes governed Program Intelligence rankings, including authorized Program Health or program-positioning products.

Publication Governance

Permanent ranking-publication boundaries
01 · Authority Stream 7 consumes official rank, criteria, confidence, verification, explanation, and version from the lawful ranking authority.
02 · Explainability Published rankings preserve WHY and authoritative intelligence references where disclosure governance allows.
03 · Visibility A valid ranking does not automatically become public. Publication and disclosure authority remain independently governed.
04 · History Ranking versions and effective periods remain preserved. Stream 7 never overwrites historical ranking truth to display a newer board.
PHNX Sports does not calculate Athletic Score, STATScore, Stars, Academic Intelligence, Development Intelligence, Program Health, Pathway Intelligence, or official rank. It publishes governed ranking products supplied by the designated authority. Athletic, Academic, Development, Verified Performance, Program, and Exposure/Viewership leaderboards remain separate intelligence classes.
