Before You Decide Important parent information
You Stay in Control You may approve all, some, or none of the choices. Approving one activity does not automatically approve the others.
Only Approved People Receive Access Your approval does not automatically confirm a coach, recruiter, counselor, or other requester. STATS-CORE must also confirm that the person is authorized.
You Can Change Your Mind Approved permissions may be updated or removed later. Removing access does not erase the secure history of earlier decisions.
Public Use Requires Permission Public profile visibility, recruiting access, messaging, and athlete media remain restricted unless the appropriate permission has been approved.
Your Decision Is Recorded STATS-CORE keeps a secure history of approvals, denials, changes, and removed permissions to help protect athletes and families.
