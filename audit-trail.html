Audit Doctrine
Receipts Are Required Every approval, denial, visibility change, communication route, report generation, and access event must produce a traceable system receipt.
No Silent Access No recruiter, evaluator, counselor, coach, parent, or program actor receives access without governance visibility.
Blocked Events Stay Visible Restricted or denied actions remain visible in the audit trail so guardians and authorized roles can understand system enforcement.
Role-Based Accountability Actions are logged by actor, role, route, timestamp, governed status, and affected athlete or program.
Crystal Report Traceability Every generated report must be traceable to its athlete, academic, program, recruiter, or combined intelligence source.
System Trust Layer The audit trail is the visible accountability layer that makes STATScore trusted infrastructure instead of an open recruiting marketplace.
