Recruiter Access Rules
Verified Recruiter ID Required Recruiters must provide validated institutional identity before athlete visibility or communication access can be activated.
Guardian Gate For Minors Recruiter-to-athlete routes involving minors must pass through parent or guardian-controlled approval channels.
Coach / Counselor Lane Provisional recruiters may be limited to coach, counselor, evaluator, or program lanes until full trust validation is complete.
Receipt Required Every visibility request, denial, approval, meeting request, and communication route must generate a system receipt.
No Off-Platform Contact Unlogged contact attempts, fake profiles, and unverified recruiter access are restricted by Multi-Box governance.
