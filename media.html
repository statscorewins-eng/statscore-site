Publication Governance

PHNX Sports Media V1.0.0
01 · Governed Stream 7 consumes governed athlete, program, professional, academic, development, ranking, pathway-safe, and Crystal intelligence. It does not reconstruct those authorities.
02 · Rights-Aware Possession of an asset does not establish publication authority. Unknown or insufficient rights and consent fail closed.
03 · Traceable Material claims, media derivatives, publication versions, authority, WHY, and receipts must remain traceable to governed lineage.
04 · Athlete-First Positive exposure may support athlete opportunity, but exposure, engagement, rankings, and media popularity must never manufacture athletic ability or recruiting outcomes.
