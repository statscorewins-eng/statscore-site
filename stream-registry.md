# PURPOSE

The Stream Registry establishes ownership boundaries for every STATS-CORE
development stream.

Every feature...
Every page...
Every engine...
Every runtime...

...must belong to one governing Stream.

Streams are organizational authority.

They prevent duplicate development,
cross-contamination,
scope drift,
and conflicting intelligence.

---

# STREAM GOVERNANCE PRINCIPLES

Every Stream shall have:

• One Mission

• One Authority

• One Scope

• Defined Deliverables

• Explicit Non-Responsibilities

No Stream may assume ownership outside its charter.
