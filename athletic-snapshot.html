Why It Matters

The Athletic Snapshot explains the measurable athletic profile: speed, explosion, agility, size/strength, position fit, verification strength, and the current development pathway. It helps athletes know what to improve, parents know what support is needed, coaches know what to verify, and recruiters know how much confidence to place in the athletic profile.
