Visibility Doctrine
Least Access Required Every role sees only what is required to perform its approved function.
Guardian Override Parent/guardian approval governs minor athlete visibility and recruiter communication activation.
Academic Separation Academic readiness is separated from athletic scoring and is exposed only to approved roles.
Verified Recruiter Filter Recruiter visibility requires identity, school affiliation, division role, and trust validation.
Crystal Report Control Reports may be generated as athlete, academic, combined, program, or recruiter-specific outputs.
Receipts Required Every visibility change must create a traceable governance receipt.
