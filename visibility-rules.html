Visibility & Disclosure

Stream 7 consumes governed enterprise visibility, rights, consent, trust, and disclosure decisions before publishing Crystal Reports, PHNX Sports media, rankings, event outputs, exposure artifacts, or other authorized enterprise publications.

Visibility Is Governed. Stream 7 does not decide enterprise role access or communication permission. It consumes the governing decision and enforces that decision within publication. Certification does not automatically establish access. Possession of information does not establish publication authority. Publication Visibility Doctrine
Least Authorized Scope Publication and presentation remain limited to the disclosure scope established by the governing authority. Stream 7 does not expand scope locally.
Role ≠ Access A Coach, Recruiter, Counselor, Evaluator, Trainer, Parent, or other role does not automatically receive universal access. Relationship, workspace, organization, athlete, purpose, and current authorization scope remain governing factors.
Certification ≠ Access PHNX Professional Certification establishes professional identity and trust where applicable. It does not create permanent athlete, team, school, league, organization, record, communication, report, or amendment access.
Guardian Authorization Guardian authorization is consumed where applicable for minor/youth athlete disclosure. It does not supersede other privacy, legal, certification, relationship, or enterprise governance controls.
Recruiting Privacy Program Match, Recruiter View, Recruiter Save, Recruiter Outreach, and Program Interest remain governed by their disclosure classification. Their existence does not authorize public media publication.
Public Media Fail-Closed PUBLIC_MEDIA requires affirmative publication/disclosure authority plus required rights and consent. Unknown, missing, expired, revoked, or insufficient authority prevents public publication.
Governance Receipt ≠ Publication Receipt The governance receipt proves the upstream visibility or disclosure determination. A Stream 7 Publication Receipt separately proves that an authorized publication operation occurred.
Historical State Is Preserved Later permission or disclosure changes must not rewrite the historical authority state under which an earlier publication was produced.
